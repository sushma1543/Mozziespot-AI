## Research Contributions

- Combines segmentation, NDWI, temporal persistence, and population exposure.
- Avoids unsupported egg-detection claims and focuses on habitat probability.
- Provides explainable AI outputs for government decision-making.
- Converts remote sensing outputs into actionable sanitation workflows.

## Evaluation Metrics

- IoU and Dice score for water segmentation
- Precision and recall for habitat classification
- Alert prioritization accuracy against field validation
- Dashboard usability and response time
